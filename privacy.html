Privacy Playbook

This page explains privacy behavior for the game in a short, readable format. It is written in plain language so players can understand what is used during gameplay and what controls remain on the device.

1) During play

The game can store local progress such as unlocked levels, score history, and gameplay preferences to keep your session consistent between launches.

2) Store and platform services

Download, updates, purchases, and account-level parental controls are managed by Apple platform services under your system settings and Apple account policies.

3) Advertising context

Some versions may include ads or reward videos. Any ad delivery logic follows iOS permissions and the privacy controls available on your device.

4) Policy updates

This text can change when the game version, platform requirements, or store disclosures change. Review this page again after major updates.

This page does not publish direct contact channels, external profile links, or any player identity fields.
